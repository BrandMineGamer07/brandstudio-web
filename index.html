FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got questions? We've got answers about our services, pricing, and delivery.

How long does it take to deliver a project?

Delivery time depends on the complexity of your order. Simple Discord setups or configurations take 24-48 hours, while full Minecraft servers or Roblox games can take 3 to 7 days.

How can I make payments?

We accept multiple payment methods including UPI / Paytm / PhonePe (for Indian clients) and PayPal / Crypto (for international clients). You can open a ticket on our Discord to discuss.

Do you provide support after delivery?

Yes! Every project comes with free post-delivery support to fix any bugs or help you configure things properly. We make sure everything runs smoothly.

How do I place an order?

Simply click on the "Order Now" button on any service or join our Discord server and open a support ticket to discuss your requirements with our team.
